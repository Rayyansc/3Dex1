About 3Dex

Made By RC

3DEX is a solo venture driven by a deep passion for Computer-Aided Design and additive manufacturing. Specializing in high-precision, print-ready CAD models, the company turns creative ideas and technical concepts into custom, functional 3D designs.

Because every project is handled end-to-end by a single dedicated creator, client interaction is direct, personalized, and meticulously tailored to ensure seamless output on any 3D printer.
